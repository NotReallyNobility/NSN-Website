Major Players

Arthur Vandelite, Rodger Oleander Daniels, and Alexandria Vandelite were the one's who kick this event off.

Tachyon acts as the primary hero, with others sprouting up around him.

The primary antagonist is The Puppeteer, who is beginning his empire.

General Information

Event Name: Paradigm Shift

Setting: Boise, Idaho. Modern era.

Summary: The Paradigm Shift event begins with Arthur Vandelite, his sister, And Rodger O. Daniels experimenting with the first full multiversal portal. Too arrogant for proper precautions, Arthur rushes ahead with the process, causing the Shockwave, a ripple across reality that frayed the fabric of space-time, allowing portals to other universes to open. These portals are unstable and hyperactive, sucking in a person and returning them, now imbued with some aspect of that universe, granting super-human powers. This is the source of most of the heroes and villains, with the first being Tachyon and Madam Malady respectively. In the background, a threat is rising, preparing for whatever plans he's setting up. Gathering promising villains, he would ensure their escape from whichever hero they were facing, remaining unseen through stealth technology in the android he exclusively ineteracted with other through. This event is the beginning, but the Puppeteer steadily works to make sure the end is in his favor. Will the heroes learn of the sinister shadow he casts, and even if they do how they can stop plans so carefully hidden? Perhaps with the help of the remorseful Arthur Vandelite, the fool who's hubris began this mess, a solution can be found.
