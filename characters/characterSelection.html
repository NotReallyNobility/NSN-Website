Singular Characters

Tachyon

First of the heroes of the new age, Alex Green plays a pivotal role setting the tone for this world. When the world he knew began to fell apart and he was granted great power, he remembered the saying's other half and embraced the responsibility. It's a lot of pressure for one man, but luckily he had help and resources granted by Elite Enterprises.

POWER: Super speed and light speed manipulation

NORMAL JOB: Carshop Mechanic

Madam Malady

First of the villains of the new age, Mallory Maladine is perhaps among the most tragic. When the world she knew began to fell apart and she was granted great power, with it came a great madness. She became a perversion of her old self, one who healed sickness doomed now to inflict. Her caring heart warped to seeing humanity itself as the disease as she attempted to cure the plague that ailed them.

POWER: Bacteria and Virus enhancement and transmission

NORMAL JOB: Doctor

Grouped Characters

The Circus of The Forsaken

Led by the eternally rotting Ringmaster, The Circus of The Forsaken is among the more disturbing groups to arise. Supernatural in origin, the group used the Shockwave's effect of weakening the uiverse's walls to breach it, and possibly find new members. The circus members treat each other well, thinking of eachother as family, but the ringmaster knows the truth. They are but food for B'Toth, the soul of this eldritch circus.

MEMBERS: Klonzy the metal jawed clown, Sandra and Samantha the elongated trapeze artists, and the Rotten Wretch the Ringmaster himself

ENEMIES: Vereran Ghost, Cryptid
